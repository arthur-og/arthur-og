## About

I'm the founder of **[Roko](https://rokoedge.com)**, where I work on engineering for **time-series systems, industrial telemetry and Edge AI**.

My work sits between software and hardware:

* time-series modeling and signal analysis;
* efficient AI inference at the edge;
* RISC-V and computer architecture;
* RTL and FPGA acceleration;
* embedded Linux and hardware–software co-design.

I like building systems where the constraints matter: **latency, memory, compute, energy and deployment close to the data**.
