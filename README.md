## About

I work at the intersection of **time-series systems, edge computing and computer architecture**. At **[Roko](https://rokoedge.com)**, I focus on industrial telemetry and on choosing the simplest method that the signal, evidence and deployment constraints support.

On the systems side, I work with **RISC-V, RTL/FPGA acceleration and embedded Linux**, with an emphasis on latency, memory, compute and energy efficiency.

## Selected Work

### [TernaryEdge-RV](https://github.com/arthur-og/TernaryEdge-RV)

**Ternary NPU + RISC-V SoC**

Hardware–software co-design for a multiplierless ternary neural-network accelerator integrated with a RISC-V SoC.

My work focuses on **hardware architecture, RTL and SoC design**, including a 64-PE ternary datapath, Wishbone integration and LiteX-based SoC integration.

`Verilog` · `RISC-V` · `LiteX` · `Wishbone` · `FPGA`

### [RISC-V 32-bit Pipeline](https://github.com/arthur-og/risk_V32b)

**Processor architecture from the ground up**

A pipelined 32-bit RISC-V processor built in Logisim Evolution, exploring datapaths, control logic and early branch resolution.

`RISC-V` · `Computer Architecture` · `Digital Logic` · `Logisim`

### [Practical Algorithms in C](https://github.com/arthur-og/practical-algorithms-c)

**Algorithms and data structures in low-level C**

Implementations of fundamental algorithms and data structures focused on clarity, computational fundamentals and explicit control over memory and execution.

`C` · `Algorithms` · `Data Structures`
