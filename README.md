### What I work on

I build systems at the intersection of **time-series intelligence, edge computing and computer architecture**.

At **Roko**, I work on engineering for time-series and industrial telemetry: understanding the signal first, establishing simple baselines, increasing model complexity when the evidence supports it, and deploying close to the data when edge constraints justify it.

On the systems side, I explore **efficient AI inference, RISC-V, RTL, FPGA acceleration and embedded Linux**.

---

### Selected work

#### [TernaryEdge-RV](https://github.com/arthur-og/TernaryEdge-RV)

Hardware–software co-design for a **multiplierless ternary NPU** integrated with a Linux-capable RISC-V SoC.

My work focuses on **hardware architecture, RTL and SoC design**, including a 64-PE ternary datapath, Wishbone integration, LiteX SoC generation and hardware verification.

`Verilog` · `RISC-V` · `LiteX` · `Wishbone` · `FPGA` · `Yosys` · `Vivado`

> Host-side RTL regression, linting and synthesis checks are passing. Physical FPGA integration and runtime measurements are ongoing.

---

#### [RISC-V 32-bit Pipeline](https://github.com/arthur-og/risk_V32b)

A pipelined 32-bit RISC-V processor designed in Logisim Evolution, exploring CPU datapaths, control logic and early branch resolution.

`RISC-V` · `Computer Architecture` · `Digital Logic` · `Logisim Evolution`

---

#### [Practical Algorithms in C](https://github.com/arthur-og/practical-algorithms-c)

Implementations of classic algorithms and data structures focused on clear C code, documentation and computational fundamentals.

`C` · `Algorithms` · `Data Structures`

### Roko

**Roko** applies engineering to time-series and edge systems.

The approach is evidence-driven: understand the signal, establish the baseline, compare alternatives, and use the simplest method that meets the problem's requirements.

When deployment constraints call for it, the same process extends to efficient inference on edge hardware.

🔗 [roko-ai.github.io](https://roko-ai.github.io)
